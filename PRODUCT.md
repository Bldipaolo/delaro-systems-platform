# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Client leaders and teams use Delaro to understand how their operation works, where work slows or fails, and which improvements are underway. Delaro consultants maintain the diagnostic model in an internal workspace.

## Product Purpose

Make operational reality legible without training clients in consulting terminology. Connect people, processes, systems, data, decisions, actions, and outcomes in a workspace that supports informed improvement work.

## Brand Personality

Architectural, editorial, restrained. Clear and confident without looking like a generic project-management tool.

## Anti-references

Avoid rounded SaaS card grids, decorative color, a flowchart-builder canvas, and labels that require Delaro-specific training to interpret.

## Design Principles

- Show real work and its consequences, not abstract dashboards.
- Lead with plain-language paths from area to process to constraint and improvement.
- Make important interventions identifiable without making every item compete for attention.
- Keep client-facing information deliberately separate from internal diagnostics.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard and screen-reader navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion preferences.
